Stu is being built as part of Steward OS. It is not yet live or sellable.

Your assistant

Stu is here to help—not take over.

A local companion being designed to help with writing, learning, research, and daily organization while keeping the person in control.

Helpful by design

Stu is intended to explain choices, prepare work, and help you move forward without turning your computer into an opaque chatbot.

Clear boundaries

Stu can propose actions. Trusted system services remain responsible for what is permitted and executed.

Local direction

The build is oriented around local operation and user control, not engagement loops or hidden provider training.

Still in development

Features, availability, and timing are not finalized. We will share details when the underlying system is properly verified.
