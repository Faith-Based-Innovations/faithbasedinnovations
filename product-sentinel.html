Steward in Sentinel

Steward is the local rules-based assistant shared across Faith Based Innovations products, not marketed as generic “AI.” In Sentinel, Steward acts as an evidence organization assistant and investigative workflow layer on your machine.

Steward can help organize evidence, surface related information, support timeline construction, highlight recurring patterns, locate supporting material, assist with summaries, and help prepare structured review materials, always oriented to what you supplied, for your review.

Steward does not provide legal advice, guaranteed outcomes, automated legal conclusions, court predictions, or attorney replacement. Sentinel organizes information; professionals and users retain responsibility for conclusions and decisions.

Exports and review materials

Sentinel is built to produce professional, modern, organized outputs: attorney-review summaries, evidence summaries, timeline packets, structured reports, printable exports, and operational review materials, with Faith Based Innovations branding integrated subtly and professionally where appropriate.
