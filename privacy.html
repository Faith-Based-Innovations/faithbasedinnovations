Privacy policy

Minimal collection, local-first products, and no surveillance-style growth tactics.

Overview

Faith Based Innovations builds desktop software intended to run primarily on your hardware. That posture shapes what we need from you online.

What we collect

We collect what is needed to operate the business: licensing, commerce, support, and security, never "just in case."

What we do not do

We do not build products around behavioral ad profiles. We do not sell attention as a product.

Where data lives

Application data is designed to remain on your device unless a feature explicitly uses an online service.

Payments

When commerce is live, payment processing will use specialized providers named on this page.

Your controls

Local data can be removed by you directly. Account-related requests follow the process published with each product.

Updates
